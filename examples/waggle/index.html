How it works

Live in an afternoon.

01

Connect your tools

ERP, help desk, books, email — OAuth in, nothing to host.

// waggle.config.ts connect('netsuite', 'zendesk', 'gmail')
02

Pick a playbook

AR chasing, reconciliation, renewals, triage — tuned templates, not blank prompts.

FAQ

Asked at every demo.

Do agents ever act without approval?
Only inside guardrails you define. Fresh workspaces default to approve-everything; most teams loosen gates as trust builds. The audit log keeps every action, forever.
What can they do on day one?
Ship with playbooks for AR chasing, ledger reconciliation, renewals, and queue triage. Connect your tools, set gates, run one night.
Which models power Waggle?
We route per-task across frontier models and our own small planners. Pin providers, or bring your own keys on the Queen plan.
Is my data used for training?
No. Workspace data stays in your workspace, encrypted at rest, deletable on request. SOC 2 Type II report under NDA.
